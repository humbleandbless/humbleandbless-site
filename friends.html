Meet the Friends

Say hello to the garden gang!

Sing along with Gracie, Honey the Bumblebee, Pip the Lamb, Ella, Lyla, baby Joy and the Garden Kids as they discover the beauty of Jesus.

  • 🌻Gracie the gardener

    Our cheerful big-sister gardener, with a daisy on her straw hat and a little ukulele. Gracie tends the prayer garden and leads the songs and prayers.

  • 🐝Honey the Bumblebee

    A round, friendly bumblebee who buzzes in with a happy “Bzzz!” Honey loves to light the way and cheer everyone on.

  • 🐑Pip the Lamb

    A fluffy little boy lamb with a soft blue collar. Pip is curious and sometimes wanders off, so he knows just how much Jesus, the Good Shepherd, cares for every lamb.

  • 🌼Ella

    Kind-hearted and full of wonder, Ella loves making new friends and caring for all of God’s beautiful creatures. You’ll often find her singing a happy song, making crafts, or curled up with a favorite Bible story.

  • 🦋Lyla

    Ella’s little sister has long wavy blonde hair and a heart full of giggles and big questions. Lyla loves to sing, make art, and help other kids discover how much Jesus loves them.

  • ⭐Baby Joy

    Ella and Lyla’s baby sister, with a little gold star on her onesie. Joy has no lines yet, just giggles, coos and sweet sleepy smiles.

  • 🌷The Garden Kids

    The friends who play, sing and pray in the garden, and who are always ready to help someone feel welcome.
