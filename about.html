Our Story

Little hearts, big love for Jesus

How it started

Humble & Bless began with a homeschooling family who loved singing, praying and reading Bible stories together. Many of us grew up on joyful sing-along Bible videos, and we wanted our own little ones to have that same happy, faith-filled fun, rooted in the beauty of the Catholic faith.

So we started dreaming up a sunny prayer garden, a cheerful gardener named Gracie, a buzzy bumblebee, a curious little lamb, and a family of girls who love Jesus. A few songs later, Humble & Bless was born.

What we believe

Every child is loved by God. We want to help kids know Jesus, talk to Him in prayer, love Mary and the saints, and treasure the stories of the Bible, all with joy, gentleness and reverence.

Why “Humble & Bless”?

Because the best things in God’s garden grow from little, humble beginnings, and because every child is a blessing.
