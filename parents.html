For Parents

Made for little ones, with grown-ups in mind

What you can expect

  • Gentle pacing, warm voices and happy songs
  • Prayers with the words on screen, so kids can pray along
  • Bible stories told simply and reverently
  • Little ways to live the faith at home, like Saint Quests and Advent ideas
  • Videos set to “Made for Kids” on YouTube

Ways to use our videos

  • Morning or bedtime prayer
  • Homeschool or catechism time
  • Learning the Rosary, one decade at a time
  • Getting ready for feast days and the seasons of the Church year

Follow along

New songs, prayers and Bible adventures are always on the way. Subscribe on YouTube at @humbleandbless, and follow Humble & Bless on Facebook.
